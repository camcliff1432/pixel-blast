## Description (max 4000)
Place blocks. Clear lines. Blast the picture.

[Name] is a fresh twist on block puzzles. Every row or column you clear turns into a shooter of that color. Send your shooters around the conveyor belt, and they knock matching pixels off a pixel-art picture. Clear the whole picture to win and add it to your album.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag blocks onto the board to fill rows and columns
• Each cleared block becomes one shot of its color
• Tap a shooter to send it around the belt. It hits the first pixel it sees, but only if the colors match
• Outer pixels hide the inner ones, so plan which colors you need next
• Don't let the board jam!

FEATURES
• Dozens of hand-made pixel pictures to collect
• Combos: clear several lines at once for bonus ammo
• Single-color lines give power shots
• Boosters for tricky moments: Rainbow shooters and Shuffle
• Win streaks for bonus coins
• Endless mode for high-score chasers
• Plays offline, with no internet required
